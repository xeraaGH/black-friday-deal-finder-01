How we check a deal

Every price on this page was read on 27 November 2026 from the store named on the card, or from a deals roundup that names that store and its price. The discount is worked out from the two prices shown, so it always adds up. Each deal carries a verdict:

  • True low — the lowest price we could find for it this year, with the evidence on the card.
  • Strong deal — a solid cut on a current product.
  • Routine sale — a small cut, or a better price at another store listed here. Check before you buy.

Where the same product is cheaper at another store, the detail page compares them side by side, cheapest first. Store links open the product at that store in a new tab. Prices move during Black Friday week — always confirm at the store before you pay.
